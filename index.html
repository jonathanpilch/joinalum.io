      Questions

      Good to know

      Is Alum free?

      Yes. Browsing, filters, radars, the tracker and interview notes are free. There's no paid tier holding back the useful part.

      Where do the internships come from?

      From each company's own career site, checked every hour: the boards they post to first (Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters) and the big companies' own systems. Alum keeps internships, co-ops and new-grad roles in tech, engineering and business tracks, in the US.

      How fresh is "fresh"?

      Every posting shows the time the company published it and how long after that it showed up on Alum. When a company only publishes the day, not the time, Alum says so instead of guessing.

      How does Alum know who can apply?

      It reads each posting for the rules that rule people out: graduation window, "must return to school", PhD or grad-only, US citizenship, security clearance, no visa sponsorship, and minimum GPA. When a posting doesn't say, Alum doesn't guess, and it always shows the rule next to the role so you can check.

      Where do the "Hokies hired" numbers come from?

      Virginia Tech's First Destination Report: graduates' self-reported first jobs, by employer and major. Alum matches those employers to the companies on the board.

      What happens to my information?

      Without an account, your profile and saved internships never leave your device. If you sign in with your vt.edu email, they sync to your account so you can use them anywhere. A resume you upload to fill in your profile is read once and not stored.

      Is this run by Virginia Tech?

      No. Alum is built by Hokies, independently. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Virginia Tech.
